Methodology

About This Dashboard

Scoring Framework

Each mineral is scored across 14 dimensions derived from a comprehensive critical minerals research framework. Scores reflect supply-side concentration, demand-side pressure, India's specific vulnerability, and price volatility.

The composite score aggregates all dimensions with framework-defined weights. The Weight Customizer on the Overview page lets you apply custom weights to reflect your own analytical priorities.

Dimension Definitions

Risk Tier Thresholds

  • Critical (>55): Severe supply concentration, active export controls, no substitutes, high India exposure. Immediate strategic concern.
  • High (50–55): Significant concentration or fast-growing demand, elevated import dependence.
  • Moderate (44–50): Meaningful risks in select dimensions; manageable with proactive policy.
  • Low (<44): Distributed supply, lower criticality, or structural oversupply.
